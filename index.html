Pipeline Point Sorter

Load a pipeline DXF and a Points.csv. Points are re-ordered along the pipeline’s drawn direction (first vertex → last vertex). Everything runs in your browser.

Supports LWPOLYLINE, POLYLINE and LINE entities. Arc bulges are treated as straight chords.
Needs Easting and Northing columns; Elevation is carried along.
Polylines from this file are drawn behind the route on the map, in their original DXF colours.

Creates points at a fixed spacing, counting from the start of the pipeline in the current direction. Reversing the direction recalculates every point from the other end. This only needs the DXF.

Click a table row or a point on the map to highlight it. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan. Double-click a Point iD cell to rename it.
Snap to:(F3 toggles snap)
Runs entirely in your browser – your files are never uploaded.
